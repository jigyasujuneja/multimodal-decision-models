# Changelog

This file records notable changes to `multimodal-decision-models`.

## [1.3.1] - 2026-11-23

### Added
- **Cross-Language Competence Circuit Breaker and Dawid-Skene `PoE` Parity (`calibration.py`, `pkg/mdm/conformal.go`, `sdk/ts/src/calibration.ts`)**: Aligns `DomainCompetenceProfile` and `compute_dawid_skene_poe_weights` across Python, Go (`IsNegativeTransfer`, `DefaultNegativeTransferCheck`, `ComputeDawidSkenePoEWeights`), and TypeScript (`isNegativeTransfer`, `computeDawidSkenePoeWeights`) within `1e-12` IEEE-754 precision. Adds held-out `stage1_brier` and `stage2_brier` degradation guards (`brier_degradation_ratio = 2.5` when `stage2_accuracy < stage1_accuracy`) and normalizes Dawid-Skene `PoE` weights onto the convex simplex inside `CascadeDecisionOrchestrator`.
- **Log-Space Exact McNemar and Holm-Bonferroni FWER Control (`eval_suite.py`)**: Replaces linear-space `math.comb` inside `paired_mcnemar_test` with `math.lgamma` log-space summation to eliminate `OverflowError` above `1,024` discordant pairs. Adds `holm_bonferroni_adjust` for multi-suite family-wise error control with strict `NaN` and boolean input rejection.

## [1.2.0] - 2026-11-13

### Added
- **Null-Prior Debiasing (`PriDe`) and Unlabeled `SLD` Label-Shift Adaptation (`calibration.py`)**: `debias_null_prior_probabilities` divides out empty-context label-surface priors $p_k^{\text{debiased}} \propto p_k / (p_k^{\text{null}})^\gamma$ before calibration. `adapt_sld_label_shift_posteriors` runs Saerens-Latinne-Decaestecker (`SLD`) unlabeled EM test-time prior shift adaptation without target labels.
- **Finite-Sample Conformal Risk Control (`CRC`), Closed-Form Bayesian Value of Information (`VoI`), and Bayes-Optimal Epistemic Abstention (`conformal.py`)**: `calibrate_conformal_risk_control` and `evaluate_conformal_risk_decision` calibrate $\hat{\lambda} = \inf\{\lambda : \frac{n}{n+1}\hat{R}_n(\lambda) + \frac{B}{n+1} \le \alpha\}$ so $\mathbb{E}[L_{n+1}(\hat{\lambda})] \le \alpha$. `compute_bayes_value_of_information` escalates when marginal risk reduction exceeds Stage-2 cost ($\text{VoI}(x) > c_{\text{S2}}$). `select_omniscience_action` and `omniscience_abstention_loss` enforce $[-C, 0, +R]$ abstention at $p^* = C / (R + C)$.
- **Held-Out Competence Circuit Breaker and Multi-Class Dawid-Skene `PoE` Weights (`calibration.py`, `tests/test_v130_competence_and_fleet_router.py`)**: `DomainCompetenceProfile.is_negative_transfer` blocks Stage-2 escalation on domains where held-out escalation hurts Stage-1 accuracy (`+5.00 pp` and `-99.1%` cost on typed schema evaluation with `0.00 pp` loss across 11 suites). `compute_dawid_skene_poe_weights` weights multi-class log-odds relative to `1/K` chance accuracy.
- **17-Model Public Calibration, `CRC` Selective Abstention, and `Bayes VoI` Benchmark (`tests/test_v120_research_primitives.py`, `benchmarks/results/v12_research_science_report.md`)**: Benchmarks `17` open and commercial decision models across `DecisionBench` (`N = 500`), multi-task calibration (`N = 50`), and `XSTest` (`N = 360`). Selective accuracy reaches `90.0%` to `91.3%` on `DecisionBench` and `98.5%` safe-homonym auto-allow at `1.67%` false-allow risk on `XSTest`.
